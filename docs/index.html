Overnight parking waiver, in one tap

Arlington bans overnight street parking (1–7 a.m.) unless you request a waiver for the night. This sets up a button in your browser that fills in the town's waiver form with your details. You still check it over and press Submit yourself.

1 Your details

These are stored only in this browser and inside your button. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Number and street only. Abbreviations like St, Ave, Rd work best.

2 Add the button to your browser

You only do this once. If your details change, come back and make a new one.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar if it's hidden: Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows, ⌘+Shift+B on a Mac.
  2. Drag this button up onto the bookmarks bar:

    🚗 Fill parking form

  2. In Safari, tap the Share icon, then Add Bookmark. Name it Fill parking form and tap Save.
  3. Open your bookmarks (the book icon), tap Edit, then tap the bookmark you just made.
  4. Clear the address line underneath the name, paste the code, and tap Done.
  1. Copied
  2. In Chrome, tap ⋮, then the ☆ star to bookmark this page.
  3. Tap Edit on the bar that pops up. Rename it to Fill parking form, replace the URL with the code you copied, and save.
  4. To use it, type Fill parking in the address bar and tap the bookmark that shows up. Tapping it from the bookmarks list doesn't work on Android.

3 Each night you need a waiver

  1. Open the town's form: Overnight Parking Request.
  2. Tap your Fill parking form button.
  3. Check the details, then press Submit on the town's page.
About the date: the town wants the date of the morning the waiver covers (1–7 a.m.). A waiver for Saturday night uses Sunday's date. The button handles this, and you can switch to tomorrow night from the bar it shows at the top of the form.
Made by a neighbor. This isn't a Town of Arlington service. If the town changes its form, the button may stop working until it's updated. You can always fill in the form by hand.
